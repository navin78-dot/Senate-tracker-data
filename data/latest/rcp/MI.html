Race Context

For much of its history, Michigan was one of the most Republican states in the Union. In 1920, it gave Democrat James M. Cox just 22 percent of the vote; in 1924 it gave John W. Davis only 13 percent; and in 1928 it gave Al Smith 29 percent of the vote. But after the sit-down strikes of the 1930s and the unionization drives that followed, Michigan quickly became competitive. The state was split between “outstate” Michigan, which retained its Republican heritage, and Detroit, which was solidly Democratic. When the Detroit suburbs, like most northern suburbs, swung toward the Democrats in the 1990s, they took the state with them. Michigan voted Democratic for president in six straight elections, from 1992 through 2012.

Pendulums, however, are made to swing. In 2016, Donald Trump carried the state by about 11,000 votes as white working-class voters abandoned the Democrats. Since then, Michigan has been one of the most competitive states in the country. Gretchen Whitmer won the governorship by 10 points in both 2018 and 2022, and Joe Biden carried the state by three points in 2020. But Trump won it back in 2024 by about a point and a half.

Despite this, Democrats have held both Senate seats since the 2000 election. In 2024, Rep. Elissa Slotkin won the Senate seat vacated by longtime Sen. Debbie Stabenow, defeating former GOP Rep. Mike Rogers by just a third of a point. Increasingly, Democratic strength comes not from the working-class vote in Macomb County, but from the upper-middle-class vote in neighboring Oakland County. In 2026, Sen. Gary Peters is retiring, creating another potentially competitive race. Republicans once again nominated Rogers. Democrats nominated Abdul El-Sayed, the former Wayne County health director. He beat the establishment favorite, Rep. Haley Stevens, by about a point in August, with backing from Bernie Sanders and the UAW. Polling shows El-Sayed with a narrow lead, but both candidates have baggage. It’s an open question whether Rogers can get past the mid-40s, where he has generally been polling, amid an unfavorable Republican environment, but for now this remains a tossup.
